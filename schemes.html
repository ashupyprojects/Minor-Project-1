By Sector

🌾 Agriculture

  • PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) - ₹6,000 yearly income support to farmers.
  • Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) - Crop insurance scheme.
  • Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) - Irrigation and water use efficiency.
  • e-NAM (National Agriculture Market) - Online platform for transparent crop trading.
  • Soil Health Card Scheme - Testing soil for better fertility & productivity.
  • Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (RKVY) - Financial support for agricultural development.

🎓 Education

  • Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan - Holistic school education (pre-school to Class 12).
  • Mid-Day Meal Scheme - Free nutritious meal for school children.
  • National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMS) - Scholarships to Class 8+ students.
  • PM Scholarship Scheme (PMSS) - For children of ex-servicemen & police.
  • AICTE Pragati Scholarship - For girl students in technical education.
  • SWAYAM - Free online learning platform.

💼 Employment

  • MGNREGA - 100 days wage employment in rural areas.
  • Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) - Skill development and training.
  • Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY) - Rural youth skill development.
  • Atal Innovation Mission (AIM) - Startup & innovation promotion.
  • PM SVANidhi Scheme - Loan for street vendors.
  • Start-Up India & Stand-Up India - Loans & mentorship for entrepreneurs.

🏥 Health

  • Ayushman Bharat - PM Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY) - ₹5 lakh free health insurance.
  • Mission Indradhanush - Free immunization program.
  • Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY) - Safe motherhood & institutional deliveries.
  • Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA) - Free antenatal checkups.
  • Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana (RSBY) - Health insurance for BPL families.
  • National Digital Health Mission (NDHM) - Digital health ID for every citizen.

👩‍👧 Women & Child

  • Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP) - Save & educate the girl child.
  • Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY) - ₹5,000 maternity benefit.
  • One Stop Centre Scheme - Support for women in distress.
  • Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) - Anganwadi nutrition & health care.
  • POSHAN Abhiyaan - Nutrition mission to reduce stunting/malnutrition.
  • Working Women Hostel Scheme - Affordable accommodation.
